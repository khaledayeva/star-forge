## Operating Rules

- `delegate` tasks require one fresh builder sub-agent.
- Every implementation-ledger sub-agent must be spawned with High reasoning.
- Builders edit only the Files owned by their task.
- The coordinator records verification and completes tasks.
- Independent tasks may run in parallel only when their Files do not overlap.
- All existing tests remain green after every integration checkpoint.
- Final review and `done --strict` run only after the delivery contract is satisfied.
